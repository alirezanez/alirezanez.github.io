**Associated with:** Esfahan Regional Electric Company (EREC)

**Duration:** Aug 2022 – Oct 2023

Collaborated as a research team member on a comprehensive study analyzing power transmission network incidents across the Isfahan regional grid using multi-year operational data (3 years). Focused on human-factor-related failures in protection systems and applied biorhythm analysis to investigate correlations between accident occurrences and human physiological cycles (physical, emotional, and intellectual). Contributed to data analysis and co-authored findings presented at the Isfahan Regional Electricity Safety Conference (Oct. 2023/Mehr 1402).

**Skills:** HSE Management Systems, Crisis Management, Data Analysis, Power Systems, Biorhythm Analysis

**Key Contributions:**
- Analyzed power transmission network incidents across Isfahan regional grid
- Investigated human-factor-related failures in protection systems
- Applied biorhythm analysis to correlate accident patterns with human physiological cycles
- Co-authored research findings for regional safety conference
- Contributed to HSE management and crisis response strategies

**Presentation:**
- Isfahan Regional Electricity Safety Conference (Oct 2023 / Mehr 1402)
