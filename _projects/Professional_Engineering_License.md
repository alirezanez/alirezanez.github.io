Professional Engineering License from the Islamic Republic of Iran, Ministry of Roads and Urban Development. Licensed to practice as an engineer in the field of **Building Electrical Installation Engineering** at **Design Grade 3** level. This license authorizes professional engagement in the design and execution of electrical installation systems in buildings, under the regulations of the Construction Engineering and Building Control Act and its executive regulations.
